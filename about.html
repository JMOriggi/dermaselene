The Story

About DermaSelene

Where an insider's eye for beauty meets the rigour of skin science.

Shanelle Rene Gomez

She knows what the camera sees

Shanelle built her career in modelling — an industry where your first appearance is your résumé. Years of castings, editorials, and runway seasons taught her precisely what it takes to look, and feel, ready for any room you walk into.

But she wanted to understand the “why” behind the surface. A degree in communication science taught her how impressions are formed and how confidence is read. A licensed esthetics education gave her the clinical tools to shape them.

DermaSelene is the result: the high-end beauty support once reserved for the modelling world — now accessible to everyone who walks through the door.

The Career Path

Three Steps, One Standard of Care

01

Modelling

Shanelle's career began as a model signed with one of New York's top agencies — years of castings, editorials, and runway seasons that taught her the standards of the image industry from the inside.

02

Communication Science

She then earned her Bachelor's and Master's degrees in Communication Science — studying how first impressions are formed in seconds, and how appearance speaks before words do.

03

Licensed Esthetics

She completed the path with her esthetician's license from Atelier Esthétique Institute of Esthetics in New York — a school training skin professionals since 1985 — grounding every DermaSelene protocol in clinical practice.

“I spent years in a world where first impressions matter — castings, fittings, runways. I built DermaSelene so you step into your big moments with the same preparation I had backstage.”
Shanelle Rene Gomez, Founder
